    For researchers

    The tampered neurons are L13 N7956 and L14 N8949, pushed by −1 and +1 on every token it writes, thinking included: added to the MLP output along each neuron's output direction, scaled to the layer's median residual norm. Each token is also run without the push on the same text, in the same batch. “Normal AI” is that run's top choice; a word counts as changed when the push made it at least 4× likelier and that choice was a different word (point at the word for both probabilities). Red dots: for the word shown, the neurons whose change (activation with the push minus without) pushed that word's logit up most directly: the change times the neuron's output · the word's unembedding, through the final norm (direct logit attribution). The list sums that over the reply's changed words. A neuron's words are the English words its output raises most through the unembedding (logit lens).
